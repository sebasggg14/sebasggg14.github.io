Projects

Sebastian Gimenez is a Game Designer, 3D Artist, and Programmer studtying at New York University. Starting off his career in Roblox, he now specializes with character and environmental design, particularly in the 3D space.

In 2022, Sebastian worked with Forever 21 and Roblox to launch various different designs onto their catalog under the Forever 21 brand. He has also worked with various Roblox development groups, including but not limited to Royale High. Now, Sebastian maintains a robust UGC catalog in the platform, including hairs, accessories, layered clothing, and custom packages.
